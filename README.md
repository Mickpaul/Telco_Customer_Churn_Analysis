# Telco Customer Churn Analysis

## Overview
This project analyzes a Telco Customer Churn dataset to identify the key factors that influence customer churn and uncover patterns that can help improve customer retention. The project explores customer demographics, service usage, and account characteristics to generate actionable business insights.

## Objectives 🎯

- Analyze customer demographics and account information.
- Identify factors that influence customer churn.
- Discover relationships between customer services and churn.
- Build an interactive dashboard for business decision-making.
- Generate actionable insights to help reduce churn

## Tools Used 🛠️
- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Power BI

## Data Cleaning

The following preprocessing steps were performed:

- Removed missing and duplicate values
- Converted data types
- Cleaned the **Total Charges** column
- Prepared the dataset for analysis and visualization

## Key Insights 🔍
- Telco month-to-month contracts customers have the highest churn. This is because  the customers on month-to-month contracts are not locked into a one- or two      year agreement. So, if they find a better price, experience poor service, or become dissatisfied, they can cancel at the end of the month because they’ve not      developed a strong loyalty for the company.
- customers that make use of the electronic check usually have the highest churn, this is likely because many of these customers are also on month-to-month          contracts and have shorter tenures, making them more likely to switch providers.
- Telco also experiences a large number of churn customers as a result of lack of Tech support, online backup and online security services. These services are       value-added services. Customers without these services typically have a simpler relationship with the company, making it easier for them to leave when they        encounter service issues, price increases, or competitive offers
- From the data analysis, Telco customer churn is predominantly a new customer problem. Customers with less than 12 months of tenure are usually the highest         proportion of churn Factors such as month-to-month contracts, limited service adoption, and lower customer loyalty contribute to the higher churn observed among   newer customers
- Customers on month-to-month contracts contribute the greatest revenue loss because they are the most likely to churn
- Customers with fiber optic internet represent a significant source of revenue loss because they combine higher monthly charges with relatively high churn rates
- Customers using Electronic Check account for a considerable proportion of revenue loss due to their higher likelihood of churning compared with customers using    automatic payment methods

## Recommendations💡

- Encourage month-to-month customers to move to one- or two-year contracts by offering discounts or incentives.
- Improve the onboarding experience during the first 12 months to increase customer satisfaction and loyalty.
- Encourage customers to switch from Electronic Check to automatic bank transfer or automatic credit card payments by offering incentives such as discounts or       bill credits.
- Create a Bundle Tech Support, Online Backup, and Online Security into promotional packages.
- Offer free trials of these services to new customers
